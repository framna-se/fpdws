Leading or not?

16 questions you might ask a Parkinator 2000 user. Some are good. Some hand the user the answer you want. Mark every question you think is leading.

5 minutesOn your own

Tap a question to mark it as leading. Tap again to unmark. When you have been through all 16, reveal the key.

  1. A specific past event. You get what actually happened, not an opinion.
  2. Pitches the idea and asks for a prediction. People say yes to be nice.
  3. The answer is built into the question.
  4. Follows the real sequence of events past the moment you care about.
  5. Asks for evidence, not memory. Showing beats telling.
  6. Covers the whole task, including the parts you didn't know existed.
  7. Assumes they would love it and only asks how much.
  8. A tag question. It asks for agreement, not information.
  9. A real incident, with consequences and workarounds attached.
  10. Concrete and recent. Easy to answer honestly, hard to embellish.
  11. Offers the answer and closes the question to yes or no.
  12. A feature pitch dressed as a question.
  13. Asks them to speculate about other people. Nobody knows.
  14. Follows the effort. Points at the real problem.
  15. Workarounds show how much a problem actually matters.
  16. States your preference and asks them to confirm it.
