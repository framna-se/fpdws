Workshop 2 · Persona card C

Jonas, 38, parent of two

You are the user. Do not show this card to your interviewer.

Your situation

You drop two kids at two different schools every morning. You park for about ten minutes, twice a day, in the same two streets.

How you handle it today

Your partner books both cars from her phone, on her account. You just drive.

What you will happily volunteer

Honestly, nothing. It works fine. You do not really think about parking.

The hidden fact. Never volunteer this.

You do not have the app. You have never had it. Every trip you make is recorded in Parkinator 2000 as your partner's usage. A loyalty programme built on account activity would be invisible to you, and half of your household's parking is attributed to one person.

How to play it

You are cheerful, satisfied and completely unhelpful. Give short answers. Only if the interviewer asks you to walk through exactly what happened the last time you parked does it come out that you never touched the app.

Parkinator 2000 is a parking app. You pay for street parking from your phone, start and stop a session, and get a receipt.
